Neighborhood Dog Walkers

About Us

Neighborhood Dog Walkers got its start on the busy sidewalks of New York, where every walk means keeping a sharp eye out for squirrels, pigeons, and the occasional street rat a dog would love to chase. That same city-trained awareness now comes with every walk here in Melbourne, Florida.

We walk all kinds of dogs: big or small, energetic or calm, scared or aggressive, with or without prong collars. After a year of walking dogs every day, we know each one needs something a little different, and we adjust to fit your dog.

Need a little extra care? We can give your dog their medication when needed, and we'll make sure their sleeping and living area is clean before we leave.

A visit, simply

  1. Tell us about your dog.
  2. We walk at their pace.
  3. We bring them home.
